# Adding a lab

A lab is not a separate course. It is attached to its lecture and shares its
name: the lecture's number gains an **L** (for example **BIO 165L**), and its
section adds a number (**A1**, **A2**) to show which lab of that letter it is.
When you remove the lecture, its labs go with it.

## Add a lab to a lecture

1. Open the **lecture's** editor (its pencil).
2. Click **Add lab section** near the bottom.
3. The editor switches to the new lab (for example **BIO 165L A1**) so you can
   give it a **Time slot** or **Custom time** right away, then **Save changes**.

The new lab starts with **no meeting time**; if you save it that way it waits in
the strip below the grid until you schedule it.

Need a second lab for the same lecture? From a lab's editor, click **Add another
lab section** — it copies that lab's time, instructor, and seats into the next
lab (**A2**, **A3**, …) and switches you to it.

## Give the lab a time

Open the lab's own editor from its pencil (or from the strip) and choose a
**Time slot** or **Custom time** exactly as for any other course. See
[Changing a course](/guide/schedule/edit-course).

::: tip
Labs show their section as a letter plus a number — **A2** means section A's
second lab. This mirrors the registrar's own notation, so it lines up with
printed schedules.
:::
