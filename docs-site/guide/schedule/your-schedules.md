# Your schedules: create, find, and show

Everything you own or can see lives behind the **Your schedules** button at the
top left. It opens a page (press **← Back** to return).

The list is grouped into three sections:

- **Your schedules** — the ones you made.
- **Shared with you** — schedules someone added you to, either to see or to
  suggest changes on.
- **Public** — schedules anyone signed in can see.

Each row tells you who owns the schedule (**You** or **by name**), its year, and
its sharing status, for example **private · can suggest: only you**.

## Create a schedule

1. Click **＋ New schedule**.
2. Give it a **Name** (leave it blank and one is chosen for you) and, if you
   like, a **Year** such as `2026-27`.
3. Pick a **Type**:
   - **Empty** — a blank schedule you fill in yourself.
   - **Random** — a starter schedule generated from the catalog, either **All
     departments** or a **Single department**.
4. Click **Generate**.

A brand-new schedule is private to you. See
[Sharing a schedule](/guide/schedule/sharing) to open it up.

## Find a schedule that was shared with you

There are no invitation links to click. The owner adds your name, and the
schedule then appears for you:

1. Click **Your schedules**.
2. Look under **Shared with you** (someone named you) or **Public** (open to
   everyone).
3. Use the **Filter by name or owner…** box or the **All years** list to narrow
   things down.

## Show, hide, and manage

Each row has small buttons on the right:

- **Eye** — show or hide the schedule in the week. Hiding only removes it from
  your view; it does not delete anything.
- **Pencil** — edit the schedule directly (your own schedules).
- **Speech bubble** — propose changes (schedules you are allowed to suggest
  on).
- **Copy** — duplicate it, which is handy as a starting point.
- **Trash** — delete it (your own schedules).

## See one schedule or several

The **Your schedules (number)** button tells you how many schedules are shown at
once. With more than one shown, the days get busy — use **Color by schedule** to
give each its own color, or **See individual courses** to list each course
separately.
