# Checking for conflicts

A conflict is when two classes are scheduled for the same day and overlapping
times. The app reports conflicts in two places; it does not stop you from
creating one.

## One course and everything it collides with

1. Click the **Course conflicts** tab.
2. In the **Course:** box, type a course code or name and pick it.
3. The page lists its **Offerings** (each section with its time and teacher) and
   a **Conflicts** table naming the courses whose times overlap.

## A teacher who is double-booked

1. Click the **Instructor** tab.
2. Choose a teacher from the **Instructor:** list.
3. A red **Conflicts** heading means they are booked twice at once; each entry
   spells out the two classes. Otherwise you will see **No double-bookings
   detected.**

<Shot file="schedule-conflicts-instructor.png" />

## Conflicts between two departments

There is no single "department versus department" screen, but two steps get you
there:

1. On the **Grid**, click **Departments** and choose both departments. Their
   classes are highlighted in color; **overlapping colored blocks** are your
   collisions.
2. To confirm a specific one, open **Course conflicts** and look up the course;
   the **Conflicts** table lists what it hits.

::: tip
A course never conflicts with its own lab. Labs belong with their lecture, so
they are not reported as a clash.
:::
