# Downloading a CSV

A CSV is a plain spreadsheet file. The **CSV ▾** button at the top right offers
two downloads, and they are not the same thing.

<Shot file="schedule-csv.png" />

## Download summary CSV

**What you are looking at now**, as a spreadsheet. It covers the term you have
selected (Fall, Winter, or Spring) and every schedule currently shown. Each row
is one class meeting, with its department, course number, section, teacher, days,
times, and term.

For the owner, this is the schedule as it stands. For someone suggesting
changes, it includes their **not-yet-submitted draft** — because the summary
follows your screen.

## Download registrar CSV

**One schedule, all three terms**, in the official registrar layout. This is the
formal record, so it reflects only changes that have actually been accepted:

- Approved suggestions are included.
- Pending proposals are **not** included.
- Your unsaved draft is **not** included.

It is meant to line up with the registrar's own files, so it can be re-imported
without surprises.

::: tip
If a number differs between the two downloads, that is why: the summary shows
what is on your screen, the registrar copy shows what is official.
:::
