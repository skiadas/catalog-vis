# Getting around the schedule

The schedule app shows a week of classes and lets you review, adjust, and
propose changes to a teaching schedule. This guide walks through it one task at
a time. The screenshots use made-up example data, but every button and label
matches the real app.

## Signing in

When you open the app you may be asked to **Sign in** or **Work offline**.

- **Sign in** saves your schedules on the college server, so they are there next
  time and can be shared with others.
- **Work offline** keeps everything on the one computer you are using. Nothing
  is saved on the server and nothing is shared. It is meant for trying things
  out.

If you are signed in, the top right shows **Signed in as …**. If it offers
**Sign in with SSO**, that takes you to the college login you already use.

## The top of the page

- **Your schedules** (top left) opens the list of schedules you own or can see.
  The number next to it is how many are currently shown.
- The colored **pill** is the schedule you are looking at. The little icons on
  it start editing or suggesting, or hide it. See
  [Your schedules](/guide/schedule/your-schedules).
- **CSV ▾** (top right) downloads the current week or a whole schedule. See
  [Downloading a CSV](/guide/schedule/csv).
- The **?** button opens a short built-in help window.

## Three ways to look at a week

Along the toolbar, the tabs **Grid**, **Course conflicts**, and **Instructor**
are your home base:

- **Grid** is the whole week at a glance — one colored block per class meeting.
- **Course conflicts** follows one course and everything that collides with it.
- **Instructor** follows one teacher's week and flags double-bookings.

From the grid you can also click a day name or a time block to look closer. See
[The views at a glance](/guide/schedule/views).

## Fall, Winter, Spring

The **Fall**, **Winter**, and **Spring** buttons switch which term you are
looking at. A schedule holds all three separately; the app shows one at a time,
and the choice is remembered the next time you visit.

::: tip
If a course seems to be missing, check the term first — it may simply be in a
different one.
:::
