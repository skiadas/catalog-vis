# Copying courses from another schedule

If your department ran a schedule before and you want those courses in this one,
you do not have to type them in one at a time. **Copy courses…** takes a whole
department's offerings from another schedule and puts them into the one you are
editing.

## Before you start

You need an **editing** or **suggesting** session open on the schedule you want
the courses to land in — see
[Editing vs suggesting](/guide/schedule/edit-vs-suggest). The schedule you copy
*from* only has to be one you can see: your own, one shared with you, or a
public one.

## Copy the courses

1. In the session bar, click **Copy courses…**, beside **＋ Add course**.
2. Pick the **Source schedule** to copy from — for example last year's
   schedule.
3. Tick the **departments** you want to copy. Only courses from the ticked
   departments are shown.
4. Leave **Include cross-listed versions** unticked unless you also want the
   other department codes a cross-listed course appears under.
5. Click **Review N courses**. Every course is ticked by default; untick any you
   do not want, or use **Select all** / **Deselect all**. Each row is tagged
   **new** (a section not yet in your schedule) or **update** (an existing
   section whose teacher, time, seats, and title will be replaced).
6. Click **Copy N courses**.

## What "updated" means

The copy looks at the section letter (**A**, **B**, …):

- A section that is already in your schedule is **updated** to match the source
  — its teacher, time, seats, and title are replaced.
- A section that is missing is **added**.

So you can run it more than once, or after you have made some changes, without
piling up duplicates. The review step also lets you leave individual courses out
of a run.

## Two things to know

- **It copies one term at a time.** The copy follows the term you are looking
  at — **Fall**, **Winter**, or **Spring**. To copy a whole year, switch term
  and repeat.
- **Who is in charge decides what happens.** If you own the schedule, the copy
  is made straight away and you can undo it from **History**. If you are
  suggesting, the copy waits in your draft like any other change, and the owner
  approves it — with the same department limits that apply when you add a course
  by hand.

::: tip
Copying is not the same as importing a CSV. A CSV import always **makes a new
schedule**; Copy courses **adds to the schedule you are already editing**. See
[Downloading a CSV](/guide/schedule/csv).
:::
