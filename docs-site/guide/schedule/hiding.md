# Where a course can hide

Usually a course is a colored block on the grid. Here is everywhere else it can
be — and why a course you know about might seem to be missing.

## Below the grid: "No meeting times"

Under the week grid there is a strip headed **No meeting times**. It holds two
kinds of courses, both easy to overlook because they are not on the calendar:

- Courses with **no meeting time at all** — independent studies and the like.
- Courses whose time falls **outside the hours the grid shows**, such as a 6:00
  PM class. Their pattern is shown as **Custom · 6:00 PM - 6:50 PM**.

## On the grid but faint: the dashed "custom" blocks

A class that meets at an unusual time — say 2:00–3:00 PM on Monday and Wednesday
instead of one of the standard blocks — appears as a **dashed block tagged
CUSTOM**, paler than the solid ones. It is there, but it does not look like the
others. In the screenshot above, the two dashed 2:00 PM blocks are exactly this.

## The other usual suspects

- **The wrong term.** Fall, Winter, and Spring are shown one at a time. Check
  the **Fall / Winter / Spring** buttons.
- **The schedule is hidden.** Use the **eye** button on the schedule's pill or
  row to bring it back. While you edit one schedule, other shown schedules are
  dimmed but still visible.
- **It is a lab.** A lab is listed with its lecture (for example **BIO 165L**),
  not as its own course, and it may start with no meeting time — so it can be in
  the strip below the grid. See [Adding a lab](/guide/schedule/labs).
- **It is filtered out of the spotlight.** Filters dim the classes that do not
  match; they do not remove them. Click **Clear** to show everything evenly
  again.
